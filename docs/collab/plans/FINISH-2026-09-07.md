## Standing rules for the squads

- One overseer per packet, five analysts (spec, codebase, tests, security, integration), a cross-review round
  where each reads the other four against the code, then a lead that implements in its own git worktree and
  commits `agents/<ID>`. The script is saved: rerun with
  `Workflow({scriptPath: ".../workflows/scripts/sentinel-packet-squads-wf_21d914fe-53a.js"})`, editing the
  `PACKETS` array. Two waves of three ran comfortably; that is the right batch size.
- No agent runs `systemctl`, `omarchy` or `sudo`, writes under `~/.config` or `~/.local`, touches
  `tests/joint/`, adds a dependency, or pushes, merges or rebases.
- The Chief reviews every branch before it merges and applies review fixes as a separate commit, so the
  agent's tree stays legible in the history.
